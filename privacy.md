# Privacy: what warpcad.com measures

This page covers the home page at warpcad.com. It does not cover the WarpCad app itself, which runs at
app.warpcad.com and has its own page: [what the hosted app measures](https://app.warpcad.com/#/help/privacy).

## What this site keeps

This site has no accounts, no forms, no comments and no sign-in. It stores nothing of its own in your browser. Your projects never reach this site: they live in the app, in your browser or in files you
save.

## Page views, through Google Analytics

The site counts visits with Google Analytics 4. The measurement is one **page view** each time a page
loads, and nothing else: no clicks, no scrolling, no search terms and no form contents.

| What is sent | What is never sent |
|---|---|
| The site address and path (for example `https://warpcad.com/privacy.html`), your browser and operating system, screen size and language, and a rough location Google derives from your IP address | The part of the address after `#`, the query string, anything you type or click, and anything you have stored in your browser |

- **Cookies.** Google Analytics sets two first-party cookies on warpcad.com, `_ga` and `_ga_JQS0CFWW6C`,
  so it can tell one visitor from another. This site sets no cookies of its own. There is no consent banner.
  If that is a problem for you, use one of the opt-outs below.
- **IP address.** Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. It uses the address to work out a
  rough location (country and city), then discards it.
- **Google signals and ad personalisation are off.** The tag asks for no advertising features and no
  cross-device tracking. This site's measurements are kept apart from the app's: they are a separate
  Google Analytics property, so the two sites' reports never mix.
- **Nothing is measured on your own machine.** A local preview, `localhost`, a page served over plain
  `http`, and automated test browsers send nothing.

## Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives the requests your browser makes to load a page, as it
does for any site it hosts, and handles them under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
This site adds no logging of its own.

## Links to the app

The **Open WarpCad** button, and the documentation links on this page, go to app.warpcad.com. Once you are
there, the app's own page applies: it describes what the app measures, which is page views only, in the
app's own Google Analytics property.

## Opting out

Any one of these stops the page view being sent:

1. Turn on **Do Not Track** or **Global Privacy Control** in your browser. This site checks both and sends nothing.
2. Install Google's [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout), or use
   a content blocker that blocks `googletagmanager.com`.
3. Use a private window, and clear site data when you close it.
